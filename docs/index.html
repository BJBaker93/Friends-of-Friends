02 / The band

Big feelings.
Built by hand.

Friends of Friends are an Australian alternative rock band, bringing indie-rock guitars, pop melody, electronic textures and punk energy into the same room.

Rooted in South East Queensland, with connections across the Gold Coast and Brisbane, the band make their music with a hands-on approach to writing, recording and production. In their AMPED interview, they discuss taking mixing and mastering into their own hands.

Want to get a feel for the sound? The 1975, The Cure and Nine Inch Nails all appear in the band's influences and press coverage. Our listening guide follows those connections, with a few places to start.

07 / A few things to know

Meet the band.

Where are Friends of Friends from?

Friends of Friends are from South East Queensland, Australia. Coverage connects the band with both the Gold Coast and Brisbane, including AMPED’s Gold Coast interview and Mixdown’s Brisbane feature.

What kind of music do Friends of Friends make?

Australian alternative rock that brings together indie rock, pop-rock hooks, electronic production and punk energy. Start with Synthetic Flower Chainsaw and Real Life, Right Now.

Where can I listen to Friends of Friends?

Find the band on Spotify, Apple Music and YouTube, or choose a service for Synthetic Flower Chainsaw.

Are there connections to The 1975 and Sam Fender?

Yes. The 1975 helped inspire the band's beginnings, as discussed in Mixdown. An Alterwave interview describes Sam Fender and Bruce Springsteen as influences on a then-upcoming single. Read more in our listening guide.
