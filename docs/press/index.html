For writers & promoters

A quick
introduction.

Friends of Friends are an Australian alternative rock band from South East Queensland, bringing together indie-rock guitars, pop hooks, electronics and punk energy.

Releases include Real Life, Right Now (2024) and Synthetic Flower Chainsaw (2025). For interviews, press materials and bookings: contact@friendsoffriendsband.com.

Official artist profiles: Spotify, Apple Music and triple j Unearthed.
